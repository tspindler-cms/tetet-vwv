# Allowed usages under VWV’s CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license

Vietnam War Vessels is released under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0**. In plain terms: others may copy, share, and build on the pack for non-commercial purposes, as long as they credit the authors and keep derivatives under the same (or a compatible) license.

Here are four examples of what that allows:

1. **Free redistribution for players**  
   A squadron or community mirror hosts the full 3.4.0 zip on a public download page or Discord channel at no charge, with a clear credit to TeTeT / Vietnam War Vessels and a link to the license. That is ordinary non-commercial sharing.

2. **Mission and campaign packs**  
   A mission builder includes VWV ships and aircraft in a free multiplayer Vietnam campaign released on the User Files site. The campaign credits the mod, and any redistributed adapted assets (for example custom liveries derived from VWV textures) stay under CC BY-NC-SA.

3. **Derivative mods and asset remixes**  
   Another modder forks a VWV destroyer, retunes its weapons for a different year, or reuses parts of a shape/livery set in their own free DCS add-on. They attribute the original work and release their adapted material under CC BY-NC-SA (or a compatible ShareAlike license).

4. **Videos, streams, and write-ups**  
   A YouTuber or streamer records sorties with VWV carriers and riverine boats, or a blogger publishes a free installation guide with screenshots. As long as the use is not primarily for commercial gain and attribution is given where the license expects it, that kind of public showcase is allowed.
